# Code of Conduct

</div>

Pacotinho Project is open to people with different backgrounds, experiences, and skill levels.

We expect everyone to contribute in a respectful and constructive way.

## Expected behavior

* Be respectful.
* Give constructive feedback.
* Discuss ideas, code, and decisions without attacking people.
* Accept different opinions and approaches.
* Help keep discussions useful and welcoming.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following is not allowed:

* Harassment or personal attacks.
* Discrimination or hate speech.
* Threats or intimidation.
* Deliberate disruption of discussions or contributions.
* Sharing private or personal information without permission.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainers through the appropriate private communication channel.

Please include enough information for the maintainers to understand what happened.

Reports will be reviewed by the project maintainers.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to maintain a respectful environment.

The goal is to keep Pacotinho Project a place where people can work on projects together without unnecessary conflict.
